How It All Fits Together

→

Our three levels build on each other, but you can join at any stage. Can't attend a live webinar? We record every session and send it out to all sign-ups.

01

Learn from Home

Online

Join our Sunday webinars to build debate theory, argument structure, and rebuttal techniques — all from the comfort of home at your own pace.

02

Practise In Person

Oxford

Put those skills into action in weekly live debate chambers. You'll be matched with a partner, given a motion and 15 minutes of prep, and judged with supportive feedback.

03

Compete & Polish

Tournaments

Test yourself in friendly student competitions with structured rounds, awards, and comprehensive feedback from expert adjudicators. 100% optional and always fun.
