Company Policies

1. Equal Opportunities

POI is committed to equal opportunities for all participants in accordance with the Equality Act 2010.

2. Safeguarding

Welfare of participants is our highest priority. Contact: info@poi.org.uk.

3. Code of Conduct

Respectful behaviour is required at all times. Harassment results in removal.

4. Complaints

Submit complaints to info@poi.org.uk. Acknowledged within 5 working days.

5. Political Neutrality

POI is a politically neutral organisation. We do not align with or promote any political party, candidate, or specific ideology. Our focus is solely on developing debate and public speaking skills in a fair, balanced, and inclusive environment.

Data Safety Policy

UK Data Protection Act 2018 & UK GDPR compliant.

Data Retention (TTL)

Data is not retained after the event. Deleted within 30 days of each session.

Your Rights

Contact info@poi.org.uk to access, correct or delete your data.
