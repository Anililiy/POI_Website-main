# Resource Protection & Sharing Guidelines

## Rules for Adding New Resources

1. **Presentation Slides & Branded Materials (Protected)**:
   - Must be embedded in the reader modal with `#toolbar=0`.
   - No download buttons on the card or in the viewer.
   - Text selection, copying, context menu, and print/save shortcuts restricted.
   - Privacy shield active when switching windows or tabs.
   - Clean viewer without watermarks.

2. **Informal Notes & Motion Guides (Unrestricted)**:
   - Viewed in the embedded reader modal with `#toolbar=1` and `data-viewer-unrestricted="true"`.
   - Direct download circle button on the card and download/external link in the viewer header.
   - Selection, copying, printing, and saving allowed.
   - Privacy shield disabled.

3. **Ambiguity Protocol**:
   - If unsure whether a resource is branded/protected slides or informal notes, always ask the user before publishing.
