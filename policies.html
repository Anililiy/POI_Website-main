Governance & Transparency

Governing Policies

Read about POI's commitment to safety, safeguarding, equal access, and transparent governance.

Safeguarding First

POI is strictly committed to the safety and welfare of every student and volunteer.

All our trustees, directors, and regular session volunteers undergo Enhanced DBS background checks and adhere to comprehensive child protection protocols.

The welfare of our participants is our highest priority. POI provides a safe, secure, and supportive environment for all young people. All founding members, directors, and regular session volunteers are fully vetted, undergo Enhanced DBS checks, and receive child safety training.

If you have any questions or safeguarding concerns, please contact our Safeguarding Lead at: contact@poi.org.uk.

POI is committed to equal opportunities for all participants in accordance with the Equality Act 2010. We celebrate diversity and believe that every young person, regardless of their background, gender, ethnicity, religion, or ability, should have equal access to debate.

To maintain a safe and respectful learning space, respectful behaviour is mandatory. All participants and volunteers must treat everyone with courtesy, listen attentively to other viewpoints, challenge arguments rather than individuals, and refrain from any discriminatory language.

We value constructive feedback. Please submit complaints to our team at contact@poi.org.uk. All submissions are acknowledged within 5 working days and investigated by the board.

POI is a politically neutral organisation. We do not promote or align with any political party, candidate, or ideology. Our focus is strictly on developing argumentation and critical thinking skills in a fair, balanced, and evidence-based environment.

Compliant with the UK Data Protection Act 2018 and UK GDPR. Data collected during registration is used solely for safety management and workshop logistics. All session signups are permanently deleted within 30 days of the session's completion.
