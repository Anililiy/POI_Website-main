Safeguarding First

POI is dedicated to the safety, protection, and welfare of every student and volunteer.

All our directors, workshop mentors, debate judges, and session volunteers undergo Enhanced DBS background checks and adhere strictly to our child protection and safeguarding frameworks.

Establishes robust financial controls to protect the assets of POI UK, guarantee reliable recording of financial transactions, and maintain public trust. Approved by Director Vivian Okolie (FCA, ACMA, CGMA).

  • Dual Authorization (Four-Eyes Principle): Electronic transfers or payments exceeding £100 require two independent bank signatories (Treasurer and designated Trustee) agreed by a majority of the board.
  • Spending Approval Limits: Up to £50 (authorized Student Project Lead); £50 to £500 (prior written approval from Treasurer); Over £500 (majority vote of the Board of Trustees).
  • Expense Reimbursements: Pre-authorized volunteer travel reimbursed upon receipt submission, or mileage allowance at 45p per mile (up to 10,000 miles).
  • Governance & Anti-Fraud: Strict conflict of interest rules and mandatory Serious Incident Reporting to the Charity Commission in the event of financial loss or irregularities.
