Official Campaign

Empower the Next Generation of Speakers

At Power of Interest UK (POI), we believe debate is about much more than winning arguments. It is about learning to articulate complex thoughts under pressure, actively listen to diverse perspectives, challenge unexamined assumptions, and carry unshakeable self-belief into adulthood.

“Every young person should have the opportunity to express their ideas, question the world around them, and find their voice. With your support, we can make that possible.”

Debating has had a profound impact on our own journeys, and we are determined to ensure that every secondary school student — regardless of background, school type, or financial means — can experience these life-changing benefits.

Where Your Money Goes
